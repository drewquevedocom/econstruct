Building or rebuilding along the **Santa Monica and Malibu coastline** means working within a regulatory and environmental context that inland Los Angeles construction simply does not encounter. Coastal Commission review and salt-air material durability are not minor details here — they are central to how these projects are planned and priced from the start.

## Coastal Zone Review: An Additional Layer, Not a Substitute

Properties within the California Coastal Zone are subject to review beyond standard city or county building permits.

### The Coastal Development Permit process

Depending on the property's location and the local jurisdiction's Local Coastal Program status, a Coastal Development Permit may be required in addition to standard building permits — reviewed either by the local jurisdiction or by the California Coastal Commission directly.

### This review often shapes the timeline more than construction itself

For many coastal projects, the coastal permitting process is the single longest variable in the overall schedule, which is why it needs to be factored into project planning from the very beginning rather than treated as a formality.

## Why Salt Air Changes Material Specification

Proximity to the ocean introduces a durability consideration that inland Los Angeles construction does not need to address to the same degree.

### Standard fasteners and metals corrode faster

Salt-laden air accelerates corrosion in standard steel fasteners, some aluminum products, and certain finishes. Coastal-grade, corrosion-resistant materials are specified specifically to perform over the long term in this environment.

### Exterior finishes need to be selected with exposure in mind

Paint systems, wood treatments, and metal finishes all perform differently near the coast than they do further inland, which is why coastal material selection is its own specialized consideration rather than a simple upgrade from standard specifications.

## What This Means for Project Planning

Coastal construction is entirely achievable — it just requires planning for realities that inland projects do not face.

### Build coastal review into the schedule from day one

Owners planning a Santa Monica or Malibu project should expect coastal review to be a defined phase of the project timeline, with realistic time allocated rather than assumed away.

### Specify materials for the environment, not just the design

Material selection for coastal properties should account for long-term performance in a salt-air environment as a core requirement, not an afterthought layered on at the finish stage.
