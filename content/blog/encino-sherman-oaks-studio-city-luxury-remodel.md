**Encino, Sherman Oaks, and Studio City** have quietly become one of the more active luxury remodel markets in Los Angeles — not because they are new, but because homeowners here are increasingly choosing to invest in place rather than sell and move to the Westside.

Our [luxury modernization](/services/luxury-modernization) team has worked across this stretch of the San Fernando Valley for years, and the pattern is consistent: larger lots and strong home values are giving owners real room to expand and upgrade.

## What Makes This Market Different From the Westside

The single biggest structural difference between the Valley and the Westside is lot size — and it changes what is actually possible on a given property.

### More room to build

Encino, Sherman Oaks, and Studio City lots are, on average, larger than comparable Westside parcels. That extra room supports additions, ADUs, pools, and outdoor living spaces that would be difficult to fit on a smaller Beverly Hills or Brentwood lot without significant compromise.

### Older housing stock means more systems work

Many homes in this corridor were built decades ago. Electrical panels, plumbing, and structural framing were often designed for a different era of use, which means a modernization project here frequently includes systems upgrades that a newer property might not need.

## Where the Hillside Rules Still Apply

Not all of this market is flat. The northern edges of Encino and Studio City rise into hillside terrain with the same regulatory profile as other LA hillside neighborhoods.

### Geotechnical requirements do not disappear in the Valley

Hillside lots in this area require the same soils and geotechnical evaluation as hillside lots in Bel Air or the Hollywood Hills. Owners on sloped Valley parcels should not assume flat-lot rules apply.

### Grading and setback limits still shape design

Valley hillside properties are subject to the same grading quantity and height limitations that apply citywide, which affects how large an addition or new structure can realistically be.

## What Homeowners Are Actually Building

The projects we see most often in this corridor tell a clear story about how families are using their investment.

### Whole-home modernization over teardown

Given the strength of the existing lots and, in many cases, sound foundations, whole-home modernization is frequently the more efficient path compared to a full rebuild — though that determination still requires a structural assessment case by case.

### Outdoor living and ADUs

With more usable lot area than the Westside typically offers, pools, outdoor kitchens, and detached ADUs are consistently among the most requested additions in this market.
